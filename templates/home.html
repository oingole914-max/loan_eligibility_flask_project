How it works

From application details to a clear signal

Three transparent steps, powered by a single saved pipeline that is loaded once — never trained during a request.

01

Tell us about the application

Complete 11 fields covering the applicant, household income, requested loan and credit history. Financial fields are validated before submission.

02

The saved pipeline scores it

The persisted preprocessing and classification pipeline imputes and encodes the inputs exactly as it did during training, then estimates a probability.

03

Review, save and revisit

See the eligibility outcome, model confidence and a full application summary. Every prediction is stored privately in your application history.

What the model sees

Four groups of applicant information

The model uses only the same 11 fields seen during training. Applicant names are stored for your records, but are not sent to the model.

Applicant profile

  • Gender
  • Marital status
  • Dependents
  • Education
  • Self-employment

Household income

  • Applicant income
  • Co-applicant income

Entered in the same monthly unit as the training dataset.

Loan structure

  • Loan amount
  • Loan term
  • Property area

Credit signal

  • Credit history

A binary indicator from the historical dataset — no bureau connection is made.

Built for understanding

A preliminary signal — with the context to read it correctly

A prediction is a statistical estimate, not an approval or rejection. {{ app_name }} shows the probability, the model used and the exact inputs behind each result so the outcome can be examined rather than treated as a black box.

Both probability and confidence

See the estimated eligibility probability and the probability attached to the predicted class.

Saved, searchable history

Revisit past assessments, filter by outcome and review every input and timestamp.

Account-based access

Applications are visible only to their owner. Administrators have a separate, role-restricted view.

Important disclaimer

This is an academic demonstration

The model was trained on a small, historical public dataset. It may encode biases present in that data and does not assess your full financial circumstances. It must never be used as the sole basis for a real lending decision.

  • No real credit check, document verification or identity verification is performed.
  • No approval, interest rate, repayment schedule or loan offer is generated.
  • Consult a qualified lender for any real borrowing decision.
